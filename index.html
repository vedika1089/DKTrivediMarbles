Meet the Consultant

Deepak Trivedi

"60 years of expertise,
available to you."

With six decades of hands-on experience in India's marble industry, Deepak Trivedi is one of Rajasthan's most trusted voices in stone. From sourcing the finest raw marble to advising on complex architectural projects, his guidance has shaped homes, hotels, monuments, and commercial spaces across the country and abroad.

Whether you are an architect selecting materials, a developer planning a large-scale build, or a homeowner renovating a single room — Deepak brings the same depth of knowledge and personal attention to every engagement.
